Data Deletion

Last updated: 06/10/2026

1. Overview

This page explains how a business customer or an end user can request deletion of data that Prospek holds through its Meta integrations: WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

2. How to Request Deletion

Businesses using Prospek: email legal@prospek.app from the email address registered on your Prospek account, with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please state your organization name and whether you want to delete (a) data from one connected account only or (b) your whole Prospek account.

People who messaged a business that uses Prospek: email legal@prospek.app with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include the phone number or social account you messaged from and, if known, the name of the business you contacted. We may ask you to verify that the number or account is yours. You may also ask the business directly, since it controls the conversations in its Prospek workspace.

You can also reach us on WhatsApp at +62 877-8558-2370.

3. What We Delete

After a verified request, we delete the data Prospek holds from the connected WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook Messenger account, which covers:

Message content and media (text, images, documents, audio, video).

Phone numbers, profile or contact names, and social account identifiers of the people who messaged the business.

Labels, notes, reminders, and follow-ups attached to those contacts and conversations.

The stored connection to your WhatsApp Business Account, Instagram account, or Facebook Page, including the access tokens we hold.

If you request deletion of your whole account, we also delete your Prospek profile and workspace data.

What we may keep: records we must retain to meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations or to resolve disputes (for example billing records), and short-lived backup copies that are overwritten on their normal cycle. Data held by Meta is governed by Meta's own policies; see the section below.

4. Timeline

We confirm receipt of your request within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days of verifying it. We confirm by email when it is done.

5. Disconnect Your Account Yourself

From Prospek: sign in at web.prospek.app, open the channel or integration settings, and disconnect the WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook Messenger account. Prospek then stops receiving and sending messages for that account.

From Meta (WhatsApp Business): in Meta Business Suite or Meta Business Settings, open Integrations or Apps, find Prospek, and remove it from your business portfolio and WhatsApp Business Account.

From Meta (Instagram and Facebook): in Instagram, go to Settings, then Website permissions (or Apps and websites), and remove Prospek. In Facebook, go to Settings, then Business Integrations, and remove Prospek.

Disconnecting stops new data from reaching Prospek. To also delete data already stored in Prospek, send us a deletion request as described above.

6. Data Held by Meta

Meta processes messages on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook under its own terms and privacy policies. Deleting data from Prospek does not delete it from Meta, or from the devices and accounts of the people you chatted with. To manage data held by Meta, use the tools in your WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook settings or contact Meta directly.

7. Contact

Questions about data deletion: legal@prospek.app
